# 06 — Cadence: when the agent acts

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 05, 12

## Question

When does the agent consider acting, and when does it hold back? The working hypothesis is that **musical time is the heartbeat** (it considers a move at phrase boundaries, every 4, 8 or 16 cycles) and **events wake it early** (a human edit, a playback error, a judgement dropping). Settle the phrase length and whether it adapts, which events interrupt, how long it lets a change breathe before judging it, and when it deliberately does nothing. Think like a DJ / VJ: a mix needs room.

Inputs from 04 and 12: a phrase is 16 cycles (one cycle is one bar), and the 8-cycle digest covers half of one. The planned Section's disposition gates "act now?" (a drop is held), and Staleness is relative to the Section. Settle how long a change breathes before its Verdict, and whether phrase length and breathing time live in `taste/loop.yaml`.
